### C. TeraWulf Surges on $19 Billion Anthropic Data-Center Lease

TeraWulf (WULF) shares jumped and snapped a seven-session losing streak after the company announced a 20-year lease agreement with Anthropic at its Justified Data campus in Hawesville, Kentucky, expected to generate approximately $19 billion in contracted lease revenue over the initial term, backed by anticipated investment-grade credit support. The deal will deliver roughly 401 MW of critical IT capacity in phases, with initial operations targeted for the second half of 2027 and full capacity by early 2028. The deal reinforced the market's read that hyperscaler and AI-lab capital commitments to power and data-center infrastructure remain robust even as some investors have questioned the durability of AI capex.
