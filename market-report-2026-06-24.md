**Date:** June 24, 2026 (covering trading session of June 23, 2026)

## Executive Summary

US equity markets sold off sharply on Monday, June 23, as a confluence of negative catalysts triggered one of the worst single-day drawdowns in technology and semiconductor stocks in months. A Bank of America research note forecasting three Fed rate hikes in 2026, a historic crash in South Korea's KOSPI triggered by MSCI's decision to exclude Korea from its Developed Markets watchlist, and broader profit-taking in AI-related names sent the Nasdaq down over 2% and the S&P 500 down ~1.4%. The Dow Jones was relatively insulated, held up by defensive and energy sector names. Geopolitical tensions eased somewhat as US-Iran nuclear talks in Switzerland progressed, pulling oil prices modestly lower.

## 2. Summary of Major Financial News & Events

### Global Tech & AI Selloff
The dominant story of the session was a violent unwinding of leveraged AI and semiconductor positions globally. What started as an overnight rout in Asia spread rapidly to US pre-market futures, with S&P 500 futures "under significant pressure" at the open.

**Key catalyst — South Korea KOSPI Crash:**
- MSCI's annual market classification review excluded South Korea from its Developed Markets watchlist, eliminating a major bull catalyst that investors had been pricing in for months (expected passive inflows from DM funds).
- KOSPI fell approximately **8–10%** on the session, triggering circuit breakers twice.
- SK Hynix and Samsung Electronics each declined ~12%; Kioxia fell over 15%; Softbank (Japan) fell 15%.
- Japan's Nikkei 225 fell 3.6%.
- India's Nifty 50 fell a comparatively modest 0.6%.

### Bank of America Rate Hike Forecast Note
Bank of America released a research note on June 23 sharply revising its Fed outlook. BofA now forecasts **three quarter-point rate hikes in 2026**, penciling in moves in September, October, and December. This marks a dramatic shift — BofA had previously forecast rate cuts in 2026, then moved to "higher for longer," and has now shifted to outright tightening.

This note rattled rate-sensitive growth stocks and further pressured semiconductors, which trade on long-duration earnings assumptions.

### Alphabet / Google DeepMind
- Shares of **Alphabet (GOOGL)** fell approximately **5%** after Reuters reported that a prominent scientist was departing Google DeepMind to join AI startup **Anthropic**.
- The headline raised investor concerns about talent retention at Alphabet's AI division and competitive dynamics in the foundational AI race.

### US-Iran Nuclear Talks
- Oil prices eased modestly after US and Iranian officials reported "great progress" during the first round of talks in Switzerland, agreeing on a roadmap toward a final deal within 60 days.
- **Brent Crude:** settled at ~$77.70/bbl (-0.3%)
- **WTI Crude:** settled at ~$73.74/bbl (-0.2%)
- Lower energy prices provided some cushion for non-tech sectors.

## 3. Notable Analyst Reports & Market Insights

### Bank of America — Federal Reserve Outlook
- **New forecast:** Three 25bp rate hikes in 2026 (September, October, December), bringing the Fed Funds target from 3.50–3.75% → 4.25–4.50%.
- Rationale: Persistent inflation driven by energy-related costs and sticky services CPI; new Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's hawkish posture.
- Impact: Immediate repricing of rate-sensitive growth equities, particularly semiconductors and AI infrastructure names.

### Deutsche Bank — Federal Reserve Outlook
- **New forecast:** Two 25bp rate hikes in 2026 (September and December).
- Deutsche Bank joined BofA in abandoning its prior "steady rates" forecast, citing the June Fed meeting's hawkish tone and updated dot plots under Chair Warsh.
- Markets (via LSEG futures) currently price ~41bp of hikes through year-end, implying roughly a coin-flip on the third hike.

### JPMorgan — IBM (IBM)
- Upgraded IBM from Neutral → **Overweight**; price target lifted to **$291** from $270.
- IBM shares gained ~4.2–4.9% on the session, one of the best performers in the Dow, as investors rotated into traditional enterprise tech.

### What Drove Rate Moves June 23

1. **BofA Research Note (Primary Catalyst):** BofA's intraday publication of a 3-hike forecast for 2026 immediately pushed short-dated yields higher. The 2s10s spread steepened slightly as markets priced more near-term tightening.
2. **Deutsche Bank Confirmation:** Deutsche Bank's aligned (if slightly less aggressive) 2-hike forecast added corroborating weight to the repricing.
3. **Fed Chair Warsh Tone:** The Federal Reserve left rates unchanged at its June 17 meeting, but Chair Warsh's commentary struck a notably hawkish tone, citing inflation persistence and a resilient labor market. Both banks' notes explicitly referenced Warsh's posture.
4. **US-Iran Peace Talks (Partial Offset):** Progress on an Iran nuclear deal/oil release agreement eased energy-driven inflation fears modestly, providing a partial counterweight to rate pressure at the long end.
5. **Markets' Implied Pricing:** Per LSEG data, futures markets are pricing ~41bps of hikes through year-end — roughly between Deutsche Bank (50bps) and BofA's (75bps) forecasts, implying the market is not yet fully pricing BofA's most hawkish call.

## 9. Market Context & Outlook

The June 23 session represents a meaningful stress test for the AI-driven bull market that has propelled semiconductors (~+90% SOXX YTD) and broad tech to historic levels in 2026. The convergence of:
1. **Tighter Fed expectations** (BofA 3-hike forecast)
2. **A structural Asia catalyst** (KOSPI/MSCI reclassification miss)
3. **AI capex credibility questions** (concerns about ROI on massive infrastructure spend)
4. **Talent risk at Google** (DeepMind departure)

...suggests the market may be entering a period of recalibration rather than simple profit-taking.

The **PCE data on Thursday** will be the next major pivot point. If core PCE comes in above the 3.4% forecast, it will add further ammunition to the BofA rate hike thesis and likely extend selling pressure in rate-sensitive growth names.
